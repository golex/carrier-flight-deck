# Image-generation scout

Can generated raster assets (gpt-image-2.5-flare on Azure, through `tools/image.mjs`) help this Canvas 2D schematic?
I generated 7 images for an estimated **$0.545**, under the $2.50 cap. Every image was viewed. The ocean tiles were also
checked with a wrap-seam metric (mean pixel difference across the wrap edge divided by the mean neighbouring-pixel
difference; about 1.0 means seamless) and a 2x2 tiled crop viewed at 1:1 around the seam intersection.

Note: `spend.log` also contains three `cand-cinematic-*` lines written by another agent running in parallel, and
`test-ocean-low.png` was already present. Neither was generated by this scout or counted in its total.

## Findings

1. **Tileable ocean: yes.** The model honours "seamless tileable" moderately well. The dark tile wraps acceptably as is,
   and the light tiles need a cheap post-process. Always run the offset-blend fix and verify with a 2x2 tile.
   The texture is only meaningful at deck zooms (about 0.3-3 px/ft). At pattern zoom (0.02-0.03 px/ft) it would be
   sub-pixel noise, so fade it out there in favour of the radar style. Draw it with `createPattern` plus a
   `setTransform` keyed to camera and simulation time (ship speed about 25 kt is about 42 ft/s of water flowing aft)
   so scrubbing stays deterministic. Expect to tint or lower opacity to keep the austere palette.
2. **Black-background VFX sprites: yes.** Steam and afterburner came back clean on pure black and are the highest-value
   assets. They give the "carefully orchestrated chaos" spectacle that is hard to fake procedurally in Canvas 2D at 60 fps
   (one `drawImage` per particle is cheap).
3. **Transparent background works** when the API is called with `background: 'transparent'`. Composition is the
   problem, not alpha: the model cannot draw physically correct wake geometry. Keep wake and bow wave procedural
   (Kelvin arms, a widening turbulent strip, and a bow-wave curl at the stem), optionally textured with a foam tile.
4. **Reference renders of the ship are misleading.** Don't spend more on them.

## Recommendation for the real build (≈ $0.40-0.60)

Generate, then post-process (crop, luma-to-alpha, seam fix) into `public/` assets at 512 px or smaller (WebP/PNG):

- **Ocean, dark** (already good, reuse `scout-ocean-dark.png`, or regenerate at high for $0.20 if crisper ripples are wanted):
  > Seamless tileable texture, perfectly top-down orthographic view straight down at open ocean surface under flat overcast light. Dark blue-grey water (around #1B2228 to #26323a), gentle small wavelets and fine ripples, very sparse tiny whitecaps, no horizon, no perspective, no boats, no sun glint, no vignette, uniform lighting and even density across the whole frame so edges wrap seamlessly on all four sides. Muted, desaturated, low contrast.
- **Ocean, light** (reuse `scout-ocean-light.png` with the seam fix): the same prompt with "very pale cool grey-blue water (around #cfd6d8 and #c2cacd), no whitecaps … Minimal, editorial, almost monochrome."
- **Steam puff** (reuse `scout-steam.png`; optionally make 2 more variants at medium, $0.14, so particles don't repeat):
  > Game VFX sprite on a pure solid black background: a single soft billowing white steam puff, seen from directly above, round-ish cloud of dense white vapor fading to transparent wispy edges, centered with generous black margin on all sides, nothing touching the frame edges, no other objects, no ground, suitable for additive blending.
- **Afterburner plume** (reuse `scout-afterburner.png` cropped; a regen should add "the plume starts exactly at the left edge, no nozzle, no engine hardware visible").
- **Foam strip tile** (new, optional, $0.07, `background: transparent`), only as a fill texture for the procedural wake:
  > Seamless horizontally tileable strip, top-down, white churned sea foam and bubbles on a transparent background, dense in the middle band and fading to soft transparent edges top and bottom, no water colour, no objects.

Don't generate: whole-wake sprites, bow-wave sprites or ship renders.

`tools/image.mjs` doesn't pass `background`. If the build wants transparent output, add an optional 5th argument
(`transparent|opaque|auto`). The scout used a scratch copy and left the tool unedited. The rate limit is about 3
requests per minute: parallel calls returned 429, so run them in sequence or retry after 60 s.

### Seam fix (Python + PIL + numpy)

```python
a = np.asarray(Image.open(src).convert('RGB')).astype(float); h, w, _ = a.shape
r = np.roll(np.roll(a, h//2, 0), w//2, 1)
m = np.minimum(np.sin(np.linspace(0, np.pi, h))[:, None], np.sin(np.linspace(0, np.pi, w))[None, :]) ** 0.7
out = m[..., None] * a + (1 - m[..., None]) * r
```
