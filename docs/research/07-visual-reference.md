# 07 - Visual Reference: Deck, Markings, Silhouettes, Diagram Conventions, Palette

Purpose: give the developer/designer everything needed to draw a clean, austere, top-down
schematic of a Nimitz-class flight deck with F/A-18E/F and EA-18G aircraft and color-coded
crew, and to pick a restrained palette that keeps jersey colors exact and legible.

Confidence tags (same convention as the other research docs):
- **[C]** confirmed by a fetched source (often two)
- **[S]** single / secondary source (forum, hobbyist, sim community)
- **[E]** estimate from general knowledge and photo study; not found in a fetched source - verify against overhead photos before treating as fact

Public authoritative marking specs (NAVAIR 00-80T-108 *CV Visual Landing Aids*, NAVAIR
00-80T-105 *CV NATOPS*, ABH training manuals) are not freely readable online in full. Much
of the marking detail below is therefore assembled from training-manual fragments, the FCLP
(shore practice deck) standard, sim/modeling communities, and photo study. That is fine for a
*schematic*, which should be simplified anyway, but the tags show what is firm.

## 1. Deck surface

| Item | Finding | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Surface | Non-skid coating (MIL-PRF-24667 systems: epoxy + aggregate, or newer thermal-spray non-skid with a polysiloxane colour topcoat). Spec covers flight, hangar and weather decks. | [C] NAVSEA/NSRP briefing, spec listing |
| Colour | **Dark grey**, *not black*. Modelers match it to Tamiya German Grey / Vallejo Panzer Grey; no FS595 number is officially published for it (FS 36118 Gunship Gray is a common modeler guess). The NAVSEA briefing says "dark gray and VLA marking colors" are applied directly over the thermal-spray non-skid. | [C] colour family; [S] exact shade |
| Appearance in photos | Reads as a mid/dark warm-neutral grey (approx. #3E4145 to #4A4D51 under daylight). Weathering is strong: rubber streaks and black tire skids along the landing-area centerline and around the wires; oil/hydraulic stains near catapults; lighter, sun-bleached grey around parking areas; patches where non-skid was replaced look darker/fresher. | [E] photo study; [S] modeler threads |
| Pad-eyes | Recessed tie-down points in a regular grid over the whole deck (modelers paint them light grey). Visible as a faint dot grid in high-res overheads. | [S] |
| Deck edge | No raised coaming to speak of; the deck edge is bounded by **catwalks** and **safety nets** one level below, plus deck-edge sponsons, the LSO platform and gun/missile sponsons. From above the outline is the hard silhouette edge. | [E] |

Design note: a schematic deck should be one flat tone (a desaturated grey, slightly cool or
neutral), with an optional second, very subtle tone for the landing-area wear band. Do not
draw non-skid texture.

## 2. Painted markings (what to draw, and how)

### 2.1 Summary table

| Marking | Colour / pattern | Shape & placement | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Landing area edge lines** | White | Two parallel solid white lines bounding the angled landing area, from the ramp (round-down) forward to the port deck edge at the angled-deck end. | [C] white lines; [E] solid |
| **"Ladder" markings** | White | Short white crossbars ("rungs") at regular intervals along/between the landing-area edge lines, giving pilots a lineup/closure cue. Wikipedia names them "ladder lines". | [C] existence; [E] exact geometry |
| **Landing area centerline** | White, dashed; alternating sections yellow at FCLP (shore) decks | Long dashed stripe down the middle of the angled deck, aligned with the stern **drop line** lights (3-6 red lights hanging down the stern below the round-down) and recessed white centerline lights. FCLP standard: "nonretroreflective white except for the alternating sections of the centerline markings that are nonretroreflective yellow". Heavily worn/scuffed in real photos. | [C] FCLP; [C] lineup with drop line; [E] shipboard dash pattern |
| **Ramp/round-down athwartship marking** | White | Line(s) across the aft end of the landing area (the FCLP standard lists "centerline, edge, and ramp athwartship markings"). | [C] FCLP |
| **Foul line / safe parking line** | **Alternating red and white stripes** | Runs the length of the landing area on the starboard side (and defines the port side clearance too), parallel to the landing-area edge. Anything inboard of it fouls the landing area. Navy training manual: "an alternating red and white striped line that runs the length of the flight deck, known as the foul line or safe parking line". A tail sticking across it = foul deck. | [C] training manual + flashcard source |
| **Arresting wires (cross-deck pendants)** | Steel (not painted); wire supports / deck sheaves may be marked | 4 wires (3 on CVN-76 Reagan, CVN-77 Bush), numbered 1-4 aft-to-forward, roughly perpendicular to the landing centerline, spaced ~40-50 ft. Draw as thin light-grey lines across the landing area with small end sheaves. | [C] |
| **Catapult tracks** | Shuttle slot = dark line; track centerline marking white | Each catapult is a long narrow slot (shuttle track) in the deck, ~300 ft (C-13 power stroke ~309 ft). Draw as a thin dark slot plus a thin white center/shot line; the shuttle is a small block at the aft end of the stroke. | [C] 4 cats; [E] markings |
| **Catapult numbers** | White numerals | "1"-"4" painted near the aft (start) end of each track. Cat 1 = starboard bow, Cat 2 = port bow, Cat 3 = inboard waist, Cat 4 = outboard (port) waist. | [E] - verify in overhead photos |
| **Catapult lead-in / taxi lines** | Yellow | Yellow lead-in lines guide the nose wheel onto the catapult (curving into the track centerline behind the JBD). Yellow is also used for general taxi guidance lines. | [E] |
| **Jet blast deflectors (JBDs)** | Panel outline; when raised they are a slab | A rectangular panel (roughly the width of an aircraft, ~ 35-40 ft x ~14 ft) flush in the deck immediately aft of each catapult's launch position; raised ~60 deg during launch. Draw flush outline when down; draw as a thick bar with a short shadow when up. | [C] existence (Wikipedia); [E] size |
| **Jet blast danger hatching** | Red hash marks | Red diagonal hash marks designate jet blast danger zones (e.g., aft of JBDs, near deck edge). | [S] modeler forum |
| **Elevator outlines** | Red/yellow (and white) safety striping | Each deck-edge elevator is outlined with hazard striping (modelers use red+yellow decals) plus elevator-lock markings. | [S] |
| **Hull number on deck** | Large white numerals | Painted on the forward flight deck at the bow (e.g., "68"), typically also near the aft end of the landing area for approaching pilots. The island also carries the number on both sides. | [S] modeler review mentions "65" on bow; [E] aft placement |
| **Touchdown aim reference** | Varies | Sim/ship-design sources mark the hook touchdown point between wires 2 and 3. No consistent painted spot on real Nimitz decks. | [S] |
| **Helicopter spots** | White circles with lineup lines | Helo spots on the angled deck/aft starboard. Irrelevant for a jets-only sim; omit or render as faint circles. | [E] |

### 2.2 Line weights for a schematic (recommended)

Real markings are roughly 1-3 ft wide. At typical zoom (whole deck ~ 1,100 px wide = ~1 ft/px):

| Element | Stroke at 1 ft/px | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Deck outline | 1.5 px | solid, slightly lighter than deck fill |
| Landing area edges | 1.5 px | solid white @ 70% |
| Ladder rungs | 1 px, ~10 ft long, every ~50 ft | white @ 50% |
| Centerline | 1.5 px, dash 20 / gap 20 | white @ 70% |
| Foul line | 2 px, dash 8 red / 8 white | red #B8332C + white |
| Catapult slot | 1 px dark + 1 px white offset | |
| Wires | 1 px | #C9CCCF @ 80% |
| Yellow lead-ins | 1 px | #D9B42A @ 70% |

Scale line weights with zoom but clamp to a minimum of 1 device px so they never vanish.

## 4. Official diagram conventions

### 4.1 CV NATOPS / handler-style deck diagrams
- **Line art, top-down, monochrome or two-tone**: deck outline, island, elevators, catapults, landing area and wires drawn as thin black lines on white. Aircraft are small outline "spot" silhouettes, usually wings-folded, parked at angles to the deck edge.
- Labels: catapults "CAT 1-4", elevators "ELEV 1-4" (or #1-#4), wires "1-4"; parking areas named; arrows show taxi flow.
- Handler's "**Ouija board**" in Flight Deck Control: a 1/16-scale (approx.) deck plan table with cutout aircraft templates, colored pushpins/nuts/washers marking status (e.g., needs fuel, ordnance, down for maintenance). Visual trope: physical, hand-placed tokens. [C existence; S details via 99% Invisible comments]
- Ship-design references (e.g. mnvdet carrier design tool) use colored lines for: wires/barricade, red dashed "safe parking" lines, aircraft pullout envelopes. [S]

### 4.2 Navy "Rainbow Jerseys" infographic (NHHC)
- Title: "RAINBOW JERSEYS - A Guide to the color-coded wardrobe on a U.S. Navy Flight Deck", by the Naval History and Heritage Command Communication and Outreach Division. Portrait poster (2:3), Bebas Neue headline, Helvetica Neue body. Seven vertical columns, one per color, in order **Blue, Purple, Green, Yellow, Red, Brown, White**, each with a bulleted role list and a short paragraph; credits Langley (CV-1) crew for originating the system in the 1920s. [C - text extracted from the PDF]
- Role lists in that poster (useful canonical copy):
  - **Blue**: plane handlers, aircraft elevator operators, tractor drivers, phone talkers ("most predominant crew on the flight deck"; chock & chain aircraft).
  - **Purple**: aviation fuels ("Grapes", "fuelies").
  - **Green**: catapult & arresting gear personnel, air wing maintenance, ground support equipment, mass communication specialists, helicopter landing signals enlisted.
  - **Yellow**: aircraft directors, catapult & arresting gear officers, Air Boss, Mini-Boss.
  - **Red**: ordnancemen, crash & salvage, EOD.
  - **Brown**: air wing plane captains ("brake riders").
  - **White**: quality control, LSOs, air transfer officers, liquid oxygen crew, safety, medical, administrative; catapult final checkers and cat/arresting gear QA inspectors.

### 4.3 Other recurring infographic tropes
- **Row of standing sailor figures**, one per jersey color, front view, with float coat, cranial helmet, goggles; sometimes with the role stenciled on the back (jerseys carry squadron/role lettering e.g. "V-1", "FUEL", "QA", "LSO", "PC" + squadron name). [E]
- **Black-and-white checkered jersey**: squadron plane inspectors / final checkers (Sandboxx; 99% Invisible). Many charts omit it and lump inspectors under White. [S - two secondary sources]
- **"Rainbow sideboys"**: one sailor of each color lined up for VIP honors - frequent photo subject. [C Military Times gallery]
- **Popular Mechanics / Business Insider / Jalopnik** style: a photo of the deck with colored callout dots and leader lines; or a cutaway carrier with numbered hotspots.
- **Night deck**: deck lit dim red/amber, aircraft lit by wand lights - motif for "night ops" mode.

### 5.2 Minimalist vector construction (normalized: length L = 1.0, nose at y=0, tail y=1.0)

Use a 2:1.36 box (length 60 ft : span 44.7 ft -> span = 0.744 L). Mirror around centerline.

| Point (half-outline, starboard side) | x (fraction of L, from centerline) | y (fraction of L, from nose) |
|---|---|---|
| Nose tip | 0.000 | 0.000 |
| Radome shoulder | 0.030 | 0.090 |
| Canopy/LEX start (LEX blends in) | 0.045 | 0.200 |
| LEX outer curve mid | 0.090 | 0.330 |
| Wing root leading edge (LEX meets wing) | 0.115 | 0.430 |
| Dogtooth (fold region) | 0.255 | 0.540 |
| Wingtip leading edge | 0.372 | 0.610 |
| Wingtip trailing edge | 0.372 | 0.680 |
| Wing trailing edge root (nearly straight TE, slight forward sweep) | 0.105 | 0.700 |
| Stabilator root LE | 0.085 | 0.810 |
| Stabilator tip LE | 0.200 | 0.920 |
| Stabilator tip TE | 0.200 | 0.975 |
| Stabilator root TE / exhaust | 0.060 | 0.990 |
| Tail end (between nozzles) | 0.000 | 1.000 |

Vertical tails (draw as thin lines/slivers on top): root from (0.055, 0.700) to (0.065,
0.880); because canted outward, the top-down tip is offset outward to about (0.115, 0.900).
Fold line: a faint line from (0.240, 0.530) to (0.240, 0.690). These are [E] proportions
tuned from published dimensions and photos - test visually.

**Wings folded**: outer panels (outboard of x = 0.24) disappear in top-down view (they
stand vertical); render them as a thin rectangle along the fold line (width ~0.02 L) to hint
at the folded panel. Folded span ~ 0.51 L (30.6 ft).

**Level of detail**: at deck-overview zoom (aircraft ~ 40-60 px long) draw only the fill
silhouette + one darker centerline/canopy spot. At closer zoom add tails, fold line,
canopy outline and store shapes.

### 5.3 EA-18G Growler differentiation (top-down)

| Cue | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Wingtip pods** | AN/ALQ-218 receiver pods replace AIM-9 wingtip rails: blunt cylindrical pods with rounded ends, slightly longer and fatter than a Sidewinder on its rail. Strongest top-down cue. | [C] |
| Jamming pods | Up to 5 ALQ-99 (centerline low-band + mid-board high-band pods); being replaced by **ALQ-249 NGJ-MB** pods (first deployed by VAQ-133 late 2024). Typical deck loadout: 2 wing jammer pods + centerline pod, or 2 NGJ pods + tanks. Pods show as fat cylinders with a small ram-air turbine at the nose. | [C] |
| Tanks | 480-gal external tanks on inboard pylons (up to 3 incl. centerline) | [C] |
| Weapons | AGM-88 HARM on outboard pylon; AIM-120 on fuselage stations | [C] |
| Gun | M61 removed (no gun port on nose - invisible from above) | [C] |
| Crew | Always two-seat (F-model canopy) | [C] |
| Wing fences | Small fences halfway along the wings; smoother dogtooth | [S] |
| Paint | Same grey tactical scheme; squadron (VAQ) tail markings | [E] |

Simulation silhouette rule: **Super Hornet = plain wingtips (or thin missile rail);
Growler = rounded wingtip pods + 2-3 underwing pods**. Also differentiate via a tiny label
(e.g., "E", "F", "G") or tail modex.

### 5.4 Aircraft colours
US Navy tactical paint scheme: FS 36375 light ghost grey (undersides/lighter areas) and FS
36320 dark ghost grey (upper surfaces). Top-down the jets read as a medium-light cool grey,
noticeably **lighter** than the non-skid. CAG/"Rhino" special-livery jets may have colored
tails. [E, standard knowledge]

### 6.3 Accessibility (colour-vision deficiency)
- **Problem pairs**: red/green/brown collapse under deuteranopia and protanopia (~8% of men);
  blue/purple are confusable under protanopia/tritanopia; yellow/white can blend at small
  sizes in bright displays.
- **Mitigations**:
  1. **Redundant shape coding** for crew markers at all zoom levels: e.g. Yellow = triangle
     (directors "point"), Green = circle, Blue = square, Red = diamond, Brown = rounded square
     with dot, Purple = hexagon, White = circle with ring, Checkered = checker-filled circle.
     Or a single-letter glyph inside the dot at medium/close zoom.
  2. **Labels on hover/tap** and a persistent **legend** keyed by both colour and shape.
  3. **Role filter**: clicking a legend item highlights that role and dims others (most
     robust aid - works for everyone and teaches the "dance").
  4. Optional **CVD-safe mode** toggle that swaps green to teal (#1BA6A6) and red to
     vermilion/orange (#E8590C), which Okabe-Ito style palettes keep distinct; keep real
     colours as default since jersey accuracy is part of the lesson.
  5. Don't encode deck status (clear/foul) by red/green alone: pair with text "CLEAR DECK" /
     "FOUL DECK" and an icon.
  6. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (reduce camera easing; keep sim playback controllable).

## Simulation implications

1. **Deck base**: render a single flat dark-grey (#3D4044) deck polygon of the Nimitz
   outline (1,092 ft long, ~252 ft max width, angled deck 9 deg to port), on a near-black
   ocean. Optional subtle darker wear band down the landing area. No textures.
2. **Markings layer** (vector, scales with zoom, min 1 px): landing-area edge lines (white),
   ladder rungs (white), dashed centerline (white, optional yellow alternation), ramp
   athwartship line, **red/white alternating foul line** along the landing area, 4 wires (3
   for CVN-76/77) numbered 1-4 aft-to-forward ~40-50 ft apart, 4 catapult slots with white
   numerals 1-4 (1 = starboard bow, 2 = port bow, 3 = inboard waist, 4 = outboard waist),
   yellow lead-in lines, JBD outlines (animated raise/lower), red blast hatching aft of JBDs,
   striped elevator outlines (4: three starboard, one port aft), hull number at the bow.
3. **Fixed features**: island (starboard, amidships), LSO platform (port, aft, abeam wires),
   IFLOLS (port, forward of LSO platform, roughly abeam island), drop-line lights at stern.
   The lens can be a small icon whose ball/datum light state is shown in an inset.
4. **Foul-line logic is visual**: when any aircraft/tractor/person polygon crosses the foul
   line, show "FOUL DECK" status; this is the key visual for recovery tempo.
5. **Aircraft sprites**: one parametric SVG path (table in section 5.2) with variants:
   wings spread / wings folded (outer panels removed, folded span ~0.51 L), single vs twin
   cockpit (E/F), Growler wingtip pods + 2-3 underwing pods. Fill ghost grey #A9AEB3 so jets
   read lighter than the deck. Scale true-to-size (60 ft x 44.7 ft spread, 30.6 ft folded).
6. **LOD**: overview zoom = simple fill silhouettes + crew dots; close zoom = tails, fold
   line, stores, crew shapes/letters, catapult shuttle and holdback.
7. **Crew markers**: dots/figures colored with the exact jersey palette, each with a
   contrasting ring, plus shape/letter redundancy; checkered pattern for plane inspectors /
   final checkers. Include a legend that doubles as a role filter.
8. **Diagram aesthetic**: borrow the CV NATOPS/handler line-art look (thin lines, labeled
   CAT 1-4, ELEV 1-4, wires 1-4) and the NHHC "Rainbow Jerseys" column legend (Blue,
   Purple, Green, Yellow, Red, Brown, White order; condensed caps headings).
9. **Night mode** (optional): darken deck, tint markings dim, emphasize wand lights and
   aircraft nav/anti-collision lights.
10. **Accessibility**: CVD-safe toggle, no red/green-only status, reduced-motion support,
    text labels on hover.
